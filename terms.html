What AcheLog is

AcheLog is a personal record-keeping tool for pain, symptoms and medication use. It records what you enter and adds it up: tallies, averages, coverage, and a chart of what you logged.

AcheLog does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for professional medical care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, or a symptom is getting worse quickly, contact your clinician or emergency services — do not rely on AcheLog.

What AcheLog is not

AcheLog does not diagnose, does not triage, does not assess risk, and does not tell you what to take or how much. The "urgent symptoms" you can flag on an entry are shown at the top of your report so a clinician sees them; ticking one is not an assessment, and AcheLog does not evaluate it.

Decisions about your medical care should be made with a qualified healthcare professional, using your full clinical picture, not an app.
