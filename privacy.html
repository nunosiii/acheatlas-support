Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

AcheAtlas is a personal pain diary. This policy is short because there is not much to say: AcheAtlas does not collect your information.

What AcheAtlas does with your data

Everything you log in AcheAtlas (pain scores, sites, medications, notes, and anything you enter under Patient and clinic details) is stored only on your device, encrypted by iOS whenever your device is locked. AcheAtlas has:

  • No account. You never sign in, and AcheAtlas does not know who you are.
  • No server. AcheAtlas does not have a backend of its own to send data to.
  • No analytics or tracking. Nothing about your use of the app is measured or transmitted.
  • No advertising, and no data shared with advertisers.
  • No iCloud sync. Your entries stay on this device; they are not uploaded to Apple or anyone else by AcheAtlas. (Your device's own encrypted backup, to iCloud or a computer, may include the app's data the same way it includes your other apps. That backup is between you and Apple or your computer, not AcheAtlas.)

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose it. Tapping Share or Print on the Report tab hands a PDF or CSV to the system share sheet or print dialog, and you pick the destination: your clinic's portal, Mail, AirDrop, a printer. AcheAtlas does not know where it went afterward. Files generated this way are deleted from the app's temporary storage the next time you leave the app.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Fill in sleep from Apple Health" under Me, AcheAtlas asks iOS for read-only access to your sleep data, one category, to fill in last night's sleep hours on a new entry when you have not already entered them. AcheAtlas never writes to Health, and never asks for any other Health data. iOS does not tell any app whether Health access was granted or declined; if sleep never fills in, check Settings, Health, Data Access.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, AcheAtlas schedules a local notification on your device. The notification says only "Daily check-in": no health information appears on your lock screen. This is a local notification, not a push notification, so no notification service ever sees it.

Children's privacy

AcheAtlas is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, adult or child, since it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If AcheAtlas ever adds a feature that changes any of the above, for example real cross-device sync, that feature will be off by default, will have its own consent screen, and this policy will be updated first.
