AcheLog

Support & Help

AcheLog is a personal pain diary that turns your entries into a printable report for a pain management clinic. Log pain, symptoms, and medication use privately on your iPhone — nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Need help or have a question? Email raulmartinez.code@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. We typically reply within 1–2 business days.

Getting started

  • Log pain now: tap the button on the Today tab whenever your pain changes. Two quick entries on a bad day tell your clinician more than one long one.
  • Pain intake: complete the one-time intake under Me — it prints at the top of every report so your appointment can start from what's changed.
  • History: browse and search past entries by note, site, or descriptor.
  • Trends: see pain over time, with Summary and Detailed levels.
  • Report: generate a PDF or CSV to share, print, or hand to your clinic.
  • Face ID lock: turn on "Require Face ID or passcode" under Me to keep your diary private if someone else picks up your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your iPhone. AcheLog does not require an account and does not transmit your entries anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does AcheLog back up to iCloud?

No. AcheLog stays local-only by design. If you delete the app or lose your device, your data goes with it unless you keep your own device backup (e.g. an encrypted iCloud or computer backup).

Does AcheLog diagnose or score my pain?

No. AcheLog records and adds up what you enter — it does not diagnose, triage, assess risk, or compute doses. See the Terms of Use for the full medical disclaimer.

What does "Fill in sleep from Apple Health" do?

With it on, AcheLog asks for read-only access to last night's sleep hours to pre-fill that one field on a new entry. AcheLog never writes to Health and never reads anything else.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email raulmartinez.code@gmail.com with as much detail as you can, including your iOS version and steps to reproduce any issue.
